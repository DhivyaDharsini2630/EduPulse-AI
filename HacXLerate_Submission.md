# HacXLerate 2026 – Round 1 Submission Draft

## Project Title
EduPulse AI

## One-line Summary
An AI-assisted student success and early-intervention platform that predicts risk, explains why students are at risk, and recommends targeted interventions.

## Problem Addressed
Educational institutions need an early-warning mechanism that identifies students who may require academic, engagement or placement support before poor outcomes become difficult to reverse.

## Solution
EduPulse AI integrates student signals into a success score, risk category and student segment. The platform exposes institution-level dashboards and student-level 360° profiles with explainable risk drivers and recommended interventions.

## Key Features
1. Role-based login for management/admin and students.
2. Student success score dashboard.
3. Risk distribution and segmentation analytics.
4. Search and filters by student, risk and department.
5. Academic Strong / Placement Unprepared identification.
6. Explainable Student 360° profile.
7. Recommended interventions.
8. Secure server-side logout and access control.

## Technology Stack
Python, Flask, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, SQLite, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js, Gunicorn.
